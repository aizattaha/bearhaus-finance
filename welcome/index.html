Step 1 — put it on your home screen (before signing up!)

BearHaus is a web app that installs like a real one — full screen, its own icon, no app store. Install it first: your login sticks to the installed app, so signing up inside it means you sign in once, ever.

📱 iPhone / iPad

1 · open the app link from my message in Safari
2 · tap the Share button □↑ at the bottom
3 · scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add

🤖 Android

1 · open the app link from my message in Chrome
2 · tap the ⋮ menu (top right)
3 · tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app) → Add
