## 3 · How the money works (10 lines)
1. A **purchase** (category, card, payer) lives in `expenses`. It counts against a fund: its category's spending pot by default (`pot_fund_id`), or the fund it names (`fund_id`).
2. A **movement/allocation** (income in, transfer, plan write, travel send, swap leg, card payment, "money out that is not a purchase") lives in `fund_transactions_all` (view `fund_transactions`).
3. **Available** (`fund_balances`) = movements in − allocations out − purchases counted against the fund (card payments excluded).
4. **In the bank** (`fund_cash.cash_balance`) = movements − non-card purchases − card payments. **Owed on cards** = card purchases − card payments. In the bank = Available + owed.
5. A card payment is recorded once, from one or more funds (`record_card_payment` legs), optionally against a statement; it never changes Available.
6. Refunds: positive amount + `is_refund`, counted negative everywhere.
7. Statements: Desk ticks rows → `reconcile_rows` (lock); close → rows hidden from later periods; opening + ticked = closing check; payment panel proposes legs from the ticked rows (remainder → spending pot).
8. Fund-funded purchases are **not** monthly budget money: excluded from Budget sums/pace, shown as "paid from funds".
9. Splits (`expense_splits`) are derived from payer + amount; settle-up reads them.
10. Soft delete everywhere; transfers and card payments delete/restore as a unit; purge after 30 days.

## 6 · Known limitations / things that look like bugs but aren't
- **The pages carry a Content Security Policy** (sprint 3). A new outside service — a CDN script, another API, a font host — is blocked until it is added to the meta tag in *both* `app/index.html` and `desktop/index.html`; `tools/ci/check-js.mjs` refuses scripts from another site. Live partner updates (phase 2) will need `wss://<project>.supabase.co` in `connect-src`.
- **supabase-js is pinned** (2.117.3). Upgrading is a deliberate change: `app/vendor/README.md`.
- **Signing out forgets everything on the device** — cached screens and last-used picks (payer, card, fund, trip). Only the keyboard height and an unused sign-up code stay. A login the server has refused (revoked, password reset elsewhere) is signed out on the next launch, or within a minute of its access token expiring while the app is open; the sign-in card then says why.
- Exchange rates: the old `api.frankfurter.app` host now redirects without a CORS header, so browsers refuse it; the Desk asks `api.frankfurter.dev` first.
- No probes any more (sprint 3): a change that needs a new column or function ships **database first** (push, wait for both deploy jobs), app second. Both deploys are independent; an app pushed before its migration breaks for those minutes.
- `supabase db push` has twice failed on its own history insert after the SQL applied (passes 3 and phase 2). Rerun the failed job → "Remote database is up to date". The workflow prints the push log tail as an annotation.
- Deleting one side of a transfer deletes both (said plainly in the sheet).
- Activity shows a Sort move as "expense deleted + fund row added" (same id).
- No realtime: a partner's new rows appear on save/refetch (≥ 60 s away) or reload.
- A view-only or member login can no longer change the household's owner or delete it; the public app key can run only the two sign-up checks (v2 sprint 1). One side of a transfer (or a card-payment leg) can't be edited on its own — the phone shows those fields dimmed.
- Desk `Pots` (🍯) lists every active fund per country — spending pots first, then each group — with a total per country, plus the credit cards.
- The Desk's ＋ add transaction is the phone's own Quick Add in a panel (DECISIONS E28), laid out wide since 18 Oct (E38): fields left, keypad / pickers / calendar right.
- **Settings lists (18 Oct, E38):** every list (People, Funds, Payment methods, Categories) starts with a long "＋ Add …" button that opens its form; archived ones fold under "Archived …" at the bottom; a payment method's type, closing day, currency, owner, order and "offered for" sit behind its ⋯. "Payment cards" is now **Payment methods** (credit, debit, bank, prepaid, cash); a new one asks for its type and currency up front (they lock once it has history). In Funds, the trip / leftover / spending-pot pickers moved under the list ("How the funds are used").
- **Desk Transactions = the whole ledger (18 Oct, E41):** purchases, refunds, settle-ups, incomes, transfers, pot swaps, card payments, trip sends, plan months and old fund rows, one line per event (click for its rows), a kind filter; read model `ledger_page` over the view `money_lines`.
- **Dashboard drill-down (18 Oct, E40):** Spending against the plan — hover or Tab to a month for spent and planned, click it to see that month's groups, click a group for its categories, Transactions › opens the matching purchases (month · group or category · budget only).
- **Pot swap, same fund (18 Oct, E39):** pick "same fund" / "the bank pot itself" in a country's second box and that country's transfer is skipped (e.g. SG money comes from the pot that pays). One country must still move. Record, new rate and undo each check every fund's Available and In the bank against what the swap says, and refuse otherwise. Pot swap is still owner-only (its RPCs look up the household by owner, not membership).
- The Desk opens on **Dashboard** (net worth per country and in total, spending vs plan, every fund's trend — E31) and has **Settings** (the phone's own, E30, in its desk layout — E38). Spending pots' history only counts purchases from `pot_start`; earlier months show their allocations only. Phone Settings has **The Desk**, **App tutorial** and **What's new**; the Desk has a 📖 Desk tour in its menu (first visit per login runs it).
- **Owed on cards starts at the start date (E36).** A fund's purchases on a card from before `pot_start` show as already spent from the bank, and a card payment from before then counts nowhere — as on the card. What a card still owed on that day is its opening (Desk → Statements), carried by the fund chosen there. The nightly check fails if what the cards owe and what the funds owe ever disagree (apart from trip lines not sent yet, settle-ups on a card, purchases in another currency and purchases on no pot, which only one side counts).
- **The phone's confirm strip (E36):** a warning holds until *Save anyway* or *Change*; ✓ alone doesn't step past it. Refusals read in full in the same strip. A phone card payment with no statement goes to the card's oldest unpaid saved statement that ended before it; the Desk can unlink it.
- A purchase in another currency on a card shows on the card as *waiting* until its charged amount is typed on the Desk; card owed leaves it out until then (sprint 2).
- The transfer/card-payment group check **refuses** any change that leaves a group unbalanced (sprint 3). A group that was already broken can only be deleted as a unit. Production had one (the QA household's 23 Jul test transfer); its data fix ran 18 Oct, so the line reads 0.
- 7 BearHaus purchases from Feb–May 2024 carry the card name "Singapore" with no card (an old import label, not a card). They sit before `pot_start`, on no card or statement, so they move no number; an edit leaves them as they are.
- **Trips (E33):** a sent purchase can't change amount, currency, card or trip, or go to the bin, until its send is undone on the trip (words, category and date stay free); what the card charged can still be corrected on the Desk and the send follows. Binning a send undoes it; a send never comes back from the bin. A foreign purchase on a card waits on the trip until its charged amount is typed. A send that no purchase explains (from the old app) is left as it is and counted by the nightly check (warn).

- **The safety net (E37):** the Desk Dashboard's top line is green when last night's money check found nothing and today's backup ran; amber = something to look at (open "what the check found") or a backup a day late; red = a check failed, a count went up, or a job stopped running (GitHub → Actions). Only a household's owner sees it. The nightly job fails — and GitHub emails — on any 'fail' line above zero **or any 'warn' count higher than the night before**; a count that stays the same doesn't email again. GitHub switches off schedules in a public repo after 60 days without a commit: the top line turns red, and GitHub → Actions → the workflow → *Enable workflow* turns it back on.
- **Data fixes** go through `tools/ops/run-data-fix.sh` (backup first, refuses one that already ran, money check before/after). `psql` on this Mac is `/opt/homebrew/opt/libpq/bin/psql` (not on PATH); the script uses it.
- **Joining (E42):** the local stack confirms email as production does (seed logins are made confirmed through the admin API). A sign-up keeps its code in the account (`user_metadata.invite`) and the confirm link returns to `…/app/?join=<code>` — GoTrue allows any address on the Site URL's host, so no redirect entry is needed. On iOS the confirm link opens Safari (separate storage and sign-in from the Home Screen app): Safari shows the join card from the account's code; after joining there it says to open BearHaus from the Home Screen and sign in. A login whose JOIN code is still waiting can't create a household (`households_invited_guard`).
- **Statements (E42):** a card's saved statements are listed with their own dates; periods from the closing day fill only the gaps. Close is the main (blue) button only when the period has ended, every row in it is ticked and the totals agree; then it closes without asking. "Checked to" = the end of the card's last closed statement (`money_checks`); `open_before` counts earlier ended periods still open.
- **A push from the clone runs the private-data guard on every commit first** (`core.hooksPath tools/git-hooks`). If it stops a push, nothing was sent.
